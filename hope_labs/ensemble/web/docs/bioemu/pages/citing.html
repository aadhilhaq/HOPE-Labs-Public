How to cite

The sampling is not the lab's work. These are the papers and the licenses of the people whose work a run on this page actually does.

BioEmu

The samples are BioEmu's. Cite it as its authors ask:

Lewis, S., Hempel, T., Jiménez-Luna, J., Gastegger, M., Xie, Y., Foong, A. Y. K.,
Satorras, V. G., Abdin, O., Veeling, B. S., Zaporozhets, I., Chen, Y., Yang, S.,
Schneuing, A., Nigam, J., Barbero, F., Stimper, V., Campbell, A., Yim, J.,
Lienen, M., Shi, Y., Zheng, S., Schulz, H., Munir, U., Tomioka, N., Clementi, C.,
and Noé, F. Scalable emulation of protein equilibrium ensembles with
generative deep learning. Science 389, eadv9817 (2025). doi:10.1126/science.adv9817

BioEmu is released by Microsoft Research under the MIT license. The license text is inside the container image this page runs, in the package's own folder.

The alignment

Unless you named an alignment of your own, it came from the ColabFold MSA server:

Mirdita, M., Schütze, K., Moriwaki, Y., Heo, L., Ovchinnikov, S., and
Steinegger, M. ColabFold: making protein folding accessible to all.
Nature Methods 19, 679 to 682 (2022). doi:10.1038/s41592-022-01488-1

The server is a shared resource with a daily limit, which is worth knowing: it is run as a courtesy by people who are not being paid for your run.
