What a run leaves

The folder, the files in it, the numbers beside each structure, and how to take any of it somewhere else.

The run folder

demo_20261005-1830/
  submission.json              what was asked for, and what Slurm was asked
  entities.json                the rows exactly as they were given
  inputs/demo.yaml             what the job read
  msa/entity_1.csv             phase one's alignment, one per distinct protein sequence
  out/boltz_results_demo/
    predictions/demo/
      demo_model_0.cif         the best structure
      confidence_demo_model_0.json
  job.sbatch                   the job, as it was queued
  slurm_12345678.out           the log
  cache/                       everything the tool wanted to write in your home folder

The alignments are the large part. Phase one leaves several megabytes per protein sequence, and a run with a few chains in it is a few hundred megabytes before a structure is written. That is why the run folder goes in your scratch and why the page refuses to put one in your home folder.

The numbers beside each structure

Boltz-2 writes a confidence file beside each structure. The page shows four of its numbers: the confidence score Boltz-2 ranks by, the complex pLDDT, and pTM and ipTM. The pLDDT is a fraction of one in the file and is shown as a percentage, so that the two folding pages read alike.

The structures are named <name>_model_0 upwards, best first. The page shows them ranked from one, because every other ranking on these pages starts at one.

pLDDT is per residue confidence and is the one to look at first. pTM says how much of the fold is likely to be right overall, and ipTM says how much the interface between chains is likely to be right, which for a complex is usually the question you came with.

Taking the results somewhere

Two ways, because they answer different questions.

To a folder on the cluster. Give a path and press Copy the structures, or Copy the whole run. This is much faster than going through this page, and it is where the lab's work belongs. A path inside your home folder is refused, with the reason: home is 10 GB and 10,000 files, and one run is more than both.

To your own computer. The save button beside each structure, and Save the best model, ask your browser where to put the file. In Chrome you get a real folder and filename chooser; in Firefox and Safari the file goes to your downloads folder, because those browsers have no chooser to offer. A structure is a few hundred kilobytes, so this is the right way to take one model and the wrong way to take a whole run.
