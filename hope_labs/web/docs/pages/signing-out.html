Signing out

Signing out closes the pages and the connection. Anything already in the queue carries on without them.

How

Press Sign out in the HOPE Labs window, or close that window. Either one stops every tool's page server on the login node, closes the ports forwarded to them, and ends the SSH connection. The tools' tabs in your browser stop answering and can be closed. The log says Signed out, and that anything already queued carries on.

What carries on

HOPE Labs submits nothing itself. The tools submit jobs to Slurm from their own pages, and a job in the queue belongs to Slurm, not to the page that submitted it: it runs, finishes and writes its results whether or not anyone is signed in. Sign in again later and the runs are in each tool's own list and in Results.

A folding run has one step that is not a job. Boltz-2 and ColabFold fetch their alignments on the login node before anything is queued, because a compute node here has no network, and that fetch belongs to the page rather than to Slurm. It is the minutes between pressing Submit and the page saying the job was queued. Signing out inside those minutes ends it, and the run has to be submitted again; after the job is queued, signing out changes nothing.

The cost follows the same rule. Every job a tool submits runs under your account and is charged to you, whether or not you were watching it.

If the connection drops

A computer going to sleep, or a change of network, ends the SSH connection, and every tool's page with it, since each runs over that connection. Jobs already in the queue carry on. Sign in again; each tool starts afresh when you pick it.
