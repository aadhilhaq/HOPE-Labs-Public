What the messages mean

The sentences the page and the job can produce, what each one is telling you, and what to do about it.

Before anything is queued

What it saysWhat to do
the target needs hotspots: the residues a binder should touchName a few residues. A run is not queued without them, because a binder with no site to aim at will settle wherever the surface is most hydrophobic.
the target has no residue A218The number is not in the chain. The chips under the target show the range each chain actually uses, and plenty of entries do not start at 1.
the target has no chain CA chain was dropped, or the entry does not have it. The chips are the chains the file holds.
the structure has to be a .pdb fileThe job reads a target by PDB columns, so an mmCIF would be copied in and then found empty. Convert it, or give a PDB.
no amino-acid chain was found in that fileThe file has no ATOM records this run can read. A nucleic acid structure, or a file of ligands only, is not a target RFdiffusion can be aimed at.
the RCSB would not give 9ZZZThe id does not exist, or the RCSB is not answering. The message carries what it said.
home is 10 GB and 10 000 files on GracePut the run folder in your scratch. One run writes more files than home will hold.
the binder length should be a range, shortest first, of at least 4 residuesThe two boxes are the wrong way round, or one of them is empty.
a walltime Slurm takes: 12:00:00, or 2-00:00:00 for two daysSlurm's own forms only. The gpu partition allows four days at most.

When the run will not queue

What it saysWhat it means
ProteinMPNN is not unpacked at /scratch/.../dl_binder_designThe second half of the run has nothing to do the sequences with, so nothing is queued at all. The install needs putting back; this is not something the form can fix.
sbatch refused the jobSlurm's own complaint, passed through. The usual causes are an account you are not a member of, a partition that has no such card, or a walltime over the partition's limit.
there is no RFdiffusion on the clusterFrom the launcher, not the page: the install is not where it is expected. Check the path on the Tools screen.

In the log

What it saysWhat it means
none of the chains A,C are in … (it has A,B)The job was told to keep a chain the file has not got. The page checks this, so seeing it here means the structure changed under the run.
rfdiffusion: 10 backbone(s)The diffusion is finished and ProteinMPNN is starting. Normal.
design_2: skipped, cannot tell the binder from the target in A:98, B:150One design could not be split into binder and target by sequence, so it was left out rather than handed on with the two the wrong way round. One or two of these in a run is not a problem; all of them means the target's chains are not coming through unchanged.
design_4: ProteinMPNN left no sequencesProteinMPNN ran and wrote nothing for that backbone. The design is skipped. Its own output under proteinmpnn/design_4/ will say why.
wrote designs.json: 9 design(s)The run finished. Nine designs came through with both a structure and a sequence.
no design came through with a sequenceThe run finished and produced nothing usable. The lines above it say which designs were skipped and why.
CUDA out of memoryThe target and the binder together are too large for the card. A shorter binder, fewer chains, or an A100 instead of a smaller card.

Slurm states on the Runs screen

StateWhat it means
PENDINGWaiting for a card. The reason beside it is Slurm's, and Resources or Priority both mean wait.
RUNNINGOn a node. The backbones tile is the one to watch.
COMPLETEDThe job exited cleanly. Check the designs tile: a run can complete having skipped every design.
TIMEOUTOut of walltime. There is no checkpoint, so the run is lost; ask for longer or for fewer backbones.
OUT_OF_MEMORYThe host ran out, not the card. Unusual at 48 GB and worth reporting.
DRY RUNNot a Slurm state. The run folder and the job script were written and nothing was queued.
