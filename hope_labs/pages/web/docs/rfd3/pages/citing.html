How to cite

What a run used, and what to cite for it.

RFdiffusion3

Butcher J, Krishna R, Mitra R, Woodbury SM, Kim D, Brent RI, et al. Atomic functional site scaffolding in biopolymers with RFdiffusion3. Science, 15 October 2026. doi:10.1126/science.aeh2982

The code and weights are distributed through Rosetta Commons Foundry, github.com/RosettaCommons/foundry. Both are BSD 3-Clause, and the license says in terms that it covers the model weights as well as the source.

ProteinMPNN

Dauparas J, Anishchenko I, Bennett N, Bai H, Ragotte RJ, Milles LF, et al. Robust deep learning-based protein sequence design using ProteinMPNN. Science 378:49-56 (2022). doi:10.1126/science.add2187

The sequences come from the lab's existing ProteinMPNN rather than the re-implementation that ships alongside the model, whose own documentation asks that the original be used for anything being compared until the new one has been validated against it.

MM-GBSA and binder strain

For a run whose designs carry the two measures. The complexes are built in tleap and minimized with sander, both AmberTools, and scored by MMPBSA.py; PDBFixer completes residues that lack atoms; the force field is ff14SB and the solvent GB-OBC(II).

Case DA, et al. AmberTools. J. Chem. Inf. Model. 63:6183-6191 (2023). doi:10.1021/acs.jcim.3c01153

Miller BR, McGee TD, Swails JM, Homeyer N, Gohlke H, Roitberg AE. MMPBSA.py: an efficient program for end-state free energy calculations. J. Chem. Theory Comput. 8:3314-3321 (2012). doi:10.1021/ct300418h

Eastman P, et al. OpenMM 7: rapid development of high performance algorithms for molecular dynamics. PLoS Comput. Biol. 13:e1005659 (2017). doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005659 (PDBFixer is part of OpenMM.)

Maier JA, et al. ff14SB: improving the accuracy of protein side chain and backbone parameters from ff99SB. J. Chem. Theory Comput. 11:3696-3713 (2015). doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.5b00255

Onufriev A, Bashford D, Case DA. Exploring protein native states and large-scale conformational changes with a modified generalized Born model. Proteins 55:383-394 (2004). doi:10.1002/prot.20033
