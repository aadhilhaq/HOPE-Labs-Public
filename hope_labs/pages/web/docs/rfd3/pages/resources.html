Resources and submitting

One GPU job does the whole run: the diffusion and then the sequences. What follows is what that job asks Slurm for, and why.

The card and the partition

Card is which GPU to ask for. The designs are diffused in one batch, so memory is set by how many were asked for rather than by how long the run is; eight designs of a hundred residues against a small target sit comfortably on a 40 GB A100.

The partition follows the card. On Grace the A100s are in gpu and the A40s are a partition of their own, gpu-a40, so a job asking for an A40 in gpu is refused outright rather than queued. The page works it out and shows it in the What Slurm will be asked for box. Naming a partition yourself in More settings overrides that, which is there for a reservation.

Walltime

Twelve hours is the default. Write it as 12:00:00, or 2-00:00:00 for two days.

There is no checkpoint. A job that runs out of walltime loses the diffusion. Any designs already written stay on disk, but designs.json is written at the end, and Queue again on the Runs screen starts the job from the beginning.

Cores, memory and the caches

The job takes eight cores and 64 GB of host memory, and neither is a choice on the form. The box shows them so a run's provenance is complete.

Everything the job writes for itself goes to scratch rather than to a home directory: XDG_CACHE_HOME, MPLCONFIGDIR and CUDA_CACHE_PATH. A home directory on Grace runs out of files long before it runs out of space, and a cache written there is how that happens.

The checkpoint, and why a compute node cannot fetch it

The model's weights are 2.5 GB and live once in the HOPE Labs folder, at ML_programs/foundry_checkpoints. The job is pointed at that copy and told not to reach for more, because a compute node on Grace has no outbound network: a run that tried to download anything would hang rather than fail, which is the worse of the two.

If the checkpoint is ever missing the page refuses the run and says where it should be, rather than queueing a job that will fail twenty minutes in.
