Engines and force fields

Five engines run the same spec. Four of them, Amber, OpenMM, GROMACS and NAMD, run one Amber topology built by tleap; Desmond builds its own system. The lists on the Engine step follow each other: an engine offers its force fields, and a force field its waters.

ff19SB with OPC is the current Amber recommendation. A water the force field was not fitted with is refused, with the ones it was. A bilayer narrows the lists further: see Membranes.

NAMD: three-site water only

NAMD 3.0.1 cannot run a four-site water (OPC, TIP4P-Ew) from the Amber topology: it stops before the minimization. On NAMD the water list holds the three-site ones alone, so ff19SB runs with TIP3P there. Run OPC or TIP4P-Ew on Amber, OpenMM or GROMACS.

GROMACS with a four-site water

A four-site water's extra point is a virtual site, which GROMACS does not take in its GPU update, so with OPC or TIP4P-Ew the update stays on the CPU threads. Such a run is slower than its atoms say, about 360 ns/day for 33 000 atoms on an A100 against 1 190 for 26 000 in TIP3P, and its speed is estimated apart from the three-site runs. TIP3P runs entirely on the GPU.

Nucleic acids

When DNA or RNA is in the run, as the partner or as the receptor, its force field is a field of its own, and the run is refused until it is chosen. Each choice loads a DNA set and RNA's OL3 together, so a system with both is covered.

Small molecules

On the Amber-topology engines a small molecule is parameterized with GAFF2 and AM1-BCC charges, the one choice under ligand parameters. A parameter job runs antechamber and parmchk2 on a CPU node before the build, at the net charge the run carries, and its log says when parmchk2 had to guess a parameter. It is the slow step of the setup.

The net charge has to be right, because AM1-BCC with the wrong total runs to completion and is wrong in every frame:

  • a ligand with bond orders (an SDF or MOL2 file, or a ligand taken from the structure) runs as it is drawn, so its formal charges fix the net charge, and the field shows it read-only as net charge (the file's). To run another charge, load a file protonated for it;
  • a PDB ligand has no charges, so you type it;
  • either way, tick charge is right once you have checked it. Submit waits for the tick.

On Desmond the System Builder types the ligand with the run's own OPLS force field from the file's bond orders and formal charges: there is nothing to choose and no charge to confirm.

Desmond

Desmond is the one engine that builds its own system, with Schrödinger's System Builder, so a few things work differently there.

  • OPLS_2005 or OPLS4. OPLS_2005 is what the academic Desmond license covers, and the default. OPLS4 needs Schrödinger's OPLS license, which the academic license on Grace lacks: a build that asks for it stops with a license error. The page says so in OPLS4's label (needs the OPLS license), beside the choice, under Submit, and once more in a question when you press Submit.
  • The license is per user. An account not on the Desmond users list (the desmond group on Grace) stops at the build with a license error.
  • The box is rectangular. The System Builder builds an orthorhombic box with the padding as its buffer on every side, so rectangular is the one shape offered.
  • The time step is fixed: Desmond's RESPA scheme, 2/2/6 fs, without hydrogen mass repartitioning.
  • One GPU per replicate. Any more asked for would sit idle.
  • Its disulfides go to Maestro as SSBOND records, those across the split of a complex among them: Maestro's PDB reader bonds two sulfurs only when a record says so.
  • Its trajectory is converted to DCD in the production job, and cpptraj analyzes it as on the other engines.
  • Its MM-GBSA is Prime's, under the full Schrödinger suite and its license tokens: see Prime MM-GBSA on Desmond.

The box and the salt

The Solvent and ions card sets the water box around the solute.

FieldDefaultWhat it does
boxcompactcompact is a truncated octahedron, the fewest waters; cubic; or rectangular, which a bilayer and Desmond need.
padding (Å)12The least distance from any solute atom to the box's edge. Under the 10 Å cutoff the complex would see its own periodic image, so 10 is the least accepted.
saltNaClNaCl, KCl or MgCl2, added after the system is neutralized.
