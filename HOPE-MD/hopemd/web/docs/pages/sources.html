Sources

Seven ways a structure comes in, all on the Structure card. Each ends the same way: a receptor, a partner unless the run is apo, and what the import found, shown in the viewer and the checks.

Receptor and ligand files

The receptor file is a PDB. chains keeps the receptor chains you name, all of them when it is empty; a chain the file does not have stops the import, naming the chains it does. The ligand file decides what the partner is:

Ligand fileDetected as
SDF or MOL2a small molecule
PDB with DNA or RNAa nucleic acid
PDB of amino-acid chains, under 50 residuesa peptide
PDB of amino-acid chains, 50 residues or morea protein partner, analyzed as an interface
any other PDBa small molecule

A small molecule from an SDF or MOL2 file is its first molecule, with the file's bond orders and formal charges, and the import says when the file holds more than one. Hydrogens the file lacks are added as drawn, and the note says how many: the protonation state drawn is the one that runs, so check it against the pH. A PDB ligand has neither bond orders nor charges: it needs its hydrogens in the file, and on the Amber-topology engines you give its net charge. A ligand holding a metal is refused, as no ligand force field here has parameters for one.

Two more choices apply to the files:

  • crystal waters: none, those within 5 Å of the partner or ligand, or all. The waters kept go into the build after the solute, as the run's water model.
  • keep metal ions: the metal ions of the files that the builds have parameters for, as free ions where the files have them. The ions a DNA or RNA binds stay either way.

A change to chains, crystal waters or keep metal ions imports the files again, and one the import refuses goes back to what it was.

The import also takes out of the receptor file what is not simulated from it: the waters and ions other than those kept, cofactors and other ligands. A partner file loses its ions and waters other than those kept. A small molecule to simulate comes in as its own SDF or MOL2 file. Modified amino acids are mapped to their parent as Prepare maps them, and residues missing in a chain end it there: Modified residues and Chain breaks say how.

The placement check

Every pose, and every receptor with its partner, is checked for where the partner sits: the closest pair of heavy atoms, the pairs within 4.5 Å, and the pairs closer than 1.5 Å.

The note saysWhat it means
partner in contact with the receptorthe usual case: how many heavy-atom pairs lie within 4.5 Å, and the closest
heavy-atom pair(s) closer than 1.5 Å between receptor and partner the two overlap: look at the pose before you submit it
the partner is n Å from the receptor at its closest more than 6 Å apart. HOPE-MD does not dock, it simulates the pose it is given: place the partner first (with a docking program), or import a docked pose.

For a complex the check is made by Prepare, on the prepared structure.

An entry, a complex file or an AlphaFold model

These three arrive whole and go through Prepare, which cleans them and splits the receptor from the partner.

  • PDB code (RCSB) takes a PDB id, four characters with a digit first such as 4CMM, and an assembly: 1, 2 and so on as the RCSB defines them, or asu for the asymmetric unit as deposited. The entry is fetched as mmCIF, and its title, method and resolution come with it.
  • Complex file takes one PDB, mmCIF or Maestro file, gzipped or not.
  • AlphaFold takes the folder AlphaFold 3 writes (its top-ranked <job>_model.cif and a seed-<s>_sample-<n>/ folder per sample), a zip of it, or one model file: an AlphaFold DB, AlphaFold 2 or ColabFold model, or one of AlphaFold 3's. model (1 = top ranked) picks the model: 1 is the top-ranked, and the samples follow by ranking score. Its pTM and, for more than one chain, its ipTM are read, and its B-factors as pLDDT; an ipTM under 0.6 is marked as a doubtful interface before a step has run. A model from the AlphaFold Server, which writes its output terms at the top of every file, is read too, with a warning on the page and in the run's report: those terms restrict how the Server's models may be used downstream, so check that this use is allowed, or use AlphaFold 3 run yourself or the AlphaFold DB.

The import proposes which chains are the receptor and which the partner, and you can change them in receptor chains and partner chains:

The partner's class follows the chains you choose: DNA or RNA makes it a nucleic acid, protein chains a protein partner, and no partner chain an apo run. A chain cannot be on both sides.

A ligand bound in the structure

A small molecule bound in an entry, a complex file or a model can be the partner in place of a chain. When there is one to offer, bound ligand as the partner lists it by residue name, chain and number, such as BTN A 300, with its heavy atoms. Waters, monatomic ions, crystallization additives and polymer residues are not offered, and the checks list any ligand that cannot be, with the reason: it holds a metal, it is bound covalently to the polymer, or it is one residue of a ligand spread over several.

The one you pick is typed against its definition in the PDB's Chemical Component Dictionary, fetched from the RCSB at import: its bond orders and formal charges come from there. A short list of rules then sets its acids and bases for pH 7.4 (carboxylic acids, phosphates, phosphonates, sulfates and sulfonates lose a proton; aliphatic amines, amidines and guanidines gain one; anything else keeps the dictionary's form) and its hydrogens are added. The structure is imported again with it:

  • its other copies are removed, as one ligand runs per system;
  • every polymer chain Prepare keeps is the receptor, so the partner chains stay empty;
  • where it has alternate conformations, the first (A) is taken;
  • the checks give its net charge and which groups changed, and the charge waits for you to confirm it. At a run pH more than a unit from 7.4 they also say to check the groups.

A ligand with atoms missing, more atoms than its definition, or bonds that are not the definition's is refused with that reason, and the choice goes back to what it was.

Docked poses

A poseviewer holds many poses.

  • Maestro poseviewer. The first structure in the file is the receptor and each one after it a pose, in the file's order. A small-molecule pose keeps the file's bond orders and formal charges.

One run per pose

For a poseviewer a box on the Structure card, One run per pose, lists what the source holds: a poseviewer's poses with their titles. Tick what to simulate, or tick all, and press Submit one run per selection.

Each selection is imported on its own and submitted with the settings on the page: the same engine, protocol, pH and environment. Load one pose first and see that its Submit is on: the page's fields have to be right for every run. Each run is named after its peptide or pose and the engine, and the box reports each one, submitted or refused, with the reason. Up to 100 selections go at a time.

A small molecule's charge is its own pose's. The charge you confirmed on the loaded pose holds for a pose whose file gives the same charge, and for one whose file gives none; a pose whose file gives another is refused, to be loaded and confirmed on its own.

DNA and RNA

A nucleic acid can be the partner (a strand a protein binds) or the whole system (a duplex, a quadruplex or an RNA, simulated whole with no partner). Either way:

  • the run needs a nucleic-acid force field, chosen on the Engine step: see Nucleic acids;
  • the cations it binds, within 3.5 Å of it, such as the K+ in a G-quadruplex's channel or an Mg2+ holding an RNA fold, are kept where the other ions of the structure are dropped;
  • a strand with a modified nucleotide is refused: the force fields here have no parameters for one;
  • each strand starts at O5′: a 5′-terminal phosphate is dropped, as the force fields have no template for it;
  • the analysis traces nucleotides by their P and C4′ atoms where it traces amino acids by their Cα.

The run's name

The run is named from its source, its pose and the engine, such as 1BHL_amber, or 1STP_BTN_amber for the entry 1STP with its bound biotin as the partner. Type a name of your own in Run name, in the rail: letters, digits, _ . - and no spaces, up to 64 characters. Empty it to go back to the derived one. A name already submitted in the runs folder is refused.
