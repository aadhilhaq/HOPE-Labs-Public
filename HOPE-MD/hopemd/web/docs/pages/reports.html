Reports and media

A finished run reads back as a report with its methods and references, two videos, still images of its first and last frames, and, across many runs, one PDF. Every word and number in them comes from the run folder; nothing is typed.

The report

Report (PDF) on the Runs screen opens the run's report in a new tab, laid out by the module every lab tool shares, with its own bar: which sections to include, how many rows the contact and hydrogen-bond tables carry, which replicates to show, a zoom for reading, Copy the methods and references, and Save as PDF. Nothing in it is typed: every number is one the run wrote, and every caption is a sentence written from those numbers when the page is opened.

SectionWhat it holds
SummaryWhat was simulated and for how long, a verdict for each replicate, and one table of the numbers that decide the question: the receptor's RMSD, the partner's, the interface, the buried surface, the contacts kept and the MM-GBSA energy.
The systemThe source, the chains and the split, the hetero groups in the file, the partner's class, the transmembrane segments, a model's confidence, the chain breaks, a disulfide that crosses the split, the bilayer, and the built system: its atoms, waters, ions and box.
The protocolThe engine with the module line its jobs loaded and the version it reported of itself, the force fields and the water, the box and the salt, the ladder as it ran, the production, and where the jobs ran.
Did it holdRMSD against time with every replicate on it, per-residue RMSF, buried surface, the area per lipid on a bilayer run, and the radius of gyration.
The interfaceThe first frame's contacts that did not survive, the hydrogen bonds that cross the interface and how long each held, how many bonds the solute held per frame, and the residue pairs that carry the interface.
MM-GBSAEvery replicate's energy with the window, the stride, the frames scored, the SD over those frames and the SEM, then the mean of the replicates with the SD of their means, which is the error to quote. A run joined by a disulfide across the split says why it has none.
The structure as it endedThe rendered first and last frames, with and without the water, and the last frame drawn in the viewer.
What it costEvery job, where it ran and for how long, and the whole graph's time beside the estimate made at Submit.
ViewsAnything added from the viewer with add to report, each with a caption written from the run and room under it for your own words.
MethodsA paragraph in a journal's form describing the run as it ran, with numbered references: see How to cite.
AppendixEvery field of the spec, Prepare's log, the built system, the provenance of the run and of the pose it started from, the caveats that are true of this run, the job ids and module lines, and what the run folder holds.

A caption says what its table or figure shows and then only what is needed to read it: the units, which direction is better, and what the spread is over. Where one replicate ran, the captions say so instead of implying error bars the run has not got, and where MM-GBSA is averaged over frames they give the window, the stride and the spread rather than the mean alone.

A run that has not been analyzed yet still reads: the system, the protocol, the methods and the appendix are there, and the first section says that nothing has been measured. One with fewer replicates finished than it asked for says that too.

The report reads the run folder and writes nothing in it, except the views you add, which go in the run's own report/ folder. It can also be written without the page, as one file that needs no server (the pictures and the 3D view need the page, so that file leaves them out):

python3 -m hopemd.export.report <run folder>      # writes export/report.html

Adding a view

add to report on the trajectory viewer's bar puts the frame as it is drawn now into the run's report: the colors, the surfaces, the chain shown and the frame you are on. The report gives it a caption from the run's own numbers, and the line under that caption is yours to write, in the report itself. Up, Down and Take out on the view reorder it or remove it.

Videos

Video queues a job that turns the replicate shown into two videos at 1920×1080: the solute alone, and the solute in its water and ions as it was simulated. About 120 frames are cut from the production trajectory itself, drawn by VMD, rendered by POV-Ray and put together by FFmpeg, all on a CPU node, in ten to forty minutes.

They are drawn in the page's colors: the receptor gray, the partner or ligand rust, the interface teal (the receptor's residues within 4.5 Å of the partner, followed frame by frame), the water pale blue and the salt amber. A metal the build carries is a sphere in its own color, the same one the Runs viewer and the report give it, and the residues holding it are sticks colored by element, chosen again in every frame so a ligand that leaves is seen leaving. Every frame carries the production time, read from the trajectory's own clock rather than from the save interval, and a legend that names only what is on the screen. They play under the Runs screen's buttons with a link to each file, and the report shows them. The files are export/<run>_r<i>.mp4 and export/<run>_r<i>_water.mp4. A replicate with no finished production part has nothing to cut, and the button says so.

Snapshots

Still images of the first and the last frame of a replicate, the solute alone and in its water (and lipids), as PNG files in export/, drawn the same way as the videos. They are a CPU job of a few minutes, queued from the command line:

python3 -m hopemd.export.snapshots <run folder> 1      # replicate 1

The portfolio

One PDF across many runs, for a reader who will not open the page: an overview table of every run, then each run's system, protocol, results with their plots, snapshots and timing beside the estimate, then the methods paragraph of each distinct protocol with its references, and where the files are.

python3 -m hopemd.export.portfolio <runs folder> --out $SCRATCH/runs.pdf

--runs followed by run names takes only those runs; --title and --author set the heading of its first page. Without --out the PDF is written into the runs folder, named with the day's date. Make the snapshots first if the PDF should show them.

Running the commands

The three commands run from the HOPE-MD checkout with its environment, on a login node:

cd <HOPE Labs folder>/HOPE-MD/MD && . ./activate.sh

The report and the portfolio read the run folders and write a file; the snapshots are submitted to Slurm, as everything that computes is. The run folder lists the other commands that work without the page.
